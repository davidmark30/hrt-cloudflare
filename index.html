What Exactly Is HRT Therapy?

Hormone replacement therapy is a medical treatment designed to balance hormones in the body. As people age or go through certain life changes, hormone levels can shift. These shifts may lead to problems such as fatigue, mood swings, weight gain, low energy, or trouble sleeping.

HRT therapy works by giving the body the hormones it is missing. Think of it like refilling a gas tank. If your body runs low, you are going to stall. With HRT, you are topping off what is missing, and suddenly the engine starts running smoothly again.
